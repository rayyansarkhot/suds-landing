Suds SMS Program

Last updated August 1, 2026

Suds is a mobile app for logging activities with friends. We use text messages for exactly one thing: signing you in.

What we send

When you create a Suds account or sign in, we text a one-time passcode to the phone number you entered so we can verify it's yours. That is the only kind of message this program sends. We do not send marketing, promotional, or recurring messages.

Consent and frequency

  • Messages are sent only when you request one by entering your phone number on the Suds sign-in screen.
  • Frequency: one message per sign-in or sign-up attempt.
  • Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts from Suds. Because sign-in codes are the only way to access your account, opting out means you will not be able to sign in until you text START to resume. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at rayyanrsarkhot@gmail.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Phone numbers collected for sign-in are used only for authentication and finding friends who already use Suds. They are never sold or shared for marketing. See the Privacy Policy for details.
